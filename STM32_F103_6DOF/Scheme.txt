How to compile
Master board:
In dma_stepper_hal.h: #define CONTROLLER_MODE CONTROLLER_MODE_MASTER
Upload via ST-Link
Slave board:
In dma_stepper_hal.h: #define CONTROLLER_MODE CONTROLLER_MODE_SLAVE
Upload via ST-Link
Both boards use identical wiring — only the CONTROLLER_MODE define differs.
